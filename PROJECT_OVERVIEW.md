# GrowFox — Platform Overview & Core Features

> **Tagline**: Empowering the next generation of storytellers and brands through high-impact, transparent, and fund-secured collaborations.  
> **Brand Parent**: Brandfinity

---

## 1. What Problem Does GrowFox Solve?

The influencer marketing industry often suffers from a lack of trust, transparency, and structure between brands and creators:

* **For Brands**:
  * **Risk of Non-Delivery**: Brands fear paying upfront because creators may underdeliver, miss deadlines, or ghost after receiving payment.
  * **Unverified Metrics**: Brands struggle to identify authentic creators versus accounts inflated with fake engagement and bot followers.
  * **High Agency Commissions**: Traditional talent agencies charge 20%–40% in middleman commissions without providing real-time visibility.

* **For Creators**:
  * **Payment Insecurity**: Creators often create and publish content but face long payment delays (30–90 days) or complete non-payment.
  * **Scope Creep**: Brands frequently demand extra deliverables and endless revisions beyond what was originally agreed upon.
  * **Lack of Direct Access**: Creators lack a direct, streamlined way to discover and pitch to verified brands with real budgets.

---

## 2. How and Why GrowFox Solves It

GrowFox provides a dedicated collaboration and payment environment that protects both parties:

### 1. Secured Funds Protection
* **Before work begins**, the brand funds the deal, locking the campaign budget into **Secured Funds (`fundsSecured`)**.
* The creator works with complete peace of mind knowing the funds are 100% reserved.
* Funds are only released to the creator once the brand reviews and approves the submitted content deliverables.

### 2. Campaign-Driven Collaboration
* Collaborations are structured through **Campaigns**:
  1. A brand creates and posts a campaign with specific deliverables, requirements, and budget.
  2. Creators discover campaigns matching their niche and submit tailored pitches.
  3. The brand hires the best-matched creator, locks the funds, and tracks milestone progress directly within the Deal Manager.

### 3. Real Identity & Social Verification
* **Aadhaar e-KYC**: Validates identity with document verification and selfie matching to prevent fraudulent accounts.
* **Instagram OAuth**: Authenticates creator channels directly to confirm account ownership and genuine metrics.

### 4. Transparent Deal Milestones
Deals follow a structured, milestone-driven flow:
* **Pending Payment**: Brand accepts application and prepares to secure funds.
* **In Progress**: Funds are secured; creator produces content according to the brief.
* **In Review**: Creator submits deliverable links (Google Drive, unlisted links, previews).
* **Revision Requested / Approved**: Brand can request adjustments or approve the submission.
* **Completed**: Brand approves deliverables, releasing earnings to the creator's wallet.

## 3. Core Features

### Brand Portal
* **Campaign Creator**: Multi-step campaign builder to define niche, requirements, platform deliverables, and budget.
* **Creator Discovery Directory**: Search and filter creators by niche, location, follower reach, and engagement tier.
* **Applicant Review Console**: Review creator proposals, view full media kits, and hire with one click.
* **Deal Manager**: Track live deals, inspect submitted content, request revisions, and approve deliverables.
* **Financial Wallet**: Deposit funds, monitor active secured campaign budgets, and review past transaction receipts.

### Creator Portal
* **Media Kit & Profile**: Showcase bio, niche, follower statistics, expertise, and verified social media links.
* **Campaign Discovery Feed**: Browse and search active brand campaigns filtered by niche and requirements.
* **Pitch & Application Manager**: Apply to campaigns with custom proposals, terms, and turnaround times.
* **Delivery Console**: Submit completed content links directly into the active deal workspace for review.
* **Earnings Wallet**: Track secured deal earnings, view cleared balances, and submit bank withdrawal requests.

### Real-Time Communication & Collaboration
* **Integrated Chat**: Direct messaging between brands and creators for coordination, feedback, and updates.
* **Live Notifications**: Instant alerts for application status, deal milestones, revisions, and payment approvals.
